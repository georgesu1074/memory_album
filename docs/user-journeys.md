# Memory Album - Detailed User Journeys

## Journey 1: Sarah's Mom at the Reception

### Context
Sarah's mom, Linda (58), is at her daughter's wedding reception. She's not very tech-savvy but wants to share a special memory about Sarah and George.

### Step-by-Step Journey

**7:30 PM - Discovery**
- Linda notices a small card on her table with a QR code
- Card reads: "Share your memories with Sarah & George - No app needed!"
- She asks her nephew to help her scan it
- He shows her how to open the camera and scan

**7:32 PM - Landing**
- The site opens instantly in her browser
- She sees a beautiful page with Sarah & George's engagement photo
- Large, clear text says "Share a Memory with the Happy Couple"
- Three big buttons: "About Sarah" | "About George" | "About Both"

**7:33 PM - Memory Selection**
- Linda taps "About Sarah"
- Page smoothly transitions to a simple form
- She sees "Your Name" field and types "Linda (Mom)"

**7:34 PM - Writing Memory**
- Large text area appears: "Share your memory..."
- She types: "I remember when Sarah was 5, she said she would only marry someone who could make her laugh every day. George, you've exceeded all her dreams. Welcome to our family, son."
- Character count shows she's used 196 of 1000 allowed

**7:35 PM - Adding Photos**
- She notices "Add Photos (optional)" button
- Taps it and her phone's photo selector opens
- She selects 3 photos: Baby Sarah, Sarah's graduation, Sarah & George's first Christmas
- Photos appear as thumbnails with little X buttons to remove

**7:36 PM - Submission**
- She taps the large "Share Memory" button
- Loading spinner appears with text "Sending your beautiful memory..."
- Success screen appears with confetti animation
- "Thank you, Linda! Your memory has been saved forever."
- Two options appear: "Share Another Memory" | "See What Others Shared"

**7:37 PM - Browsing**
- Linda taps "See What Others Shared"
- She sees a grid of memory cards
- One catches her eye: "College Memories" with 5 contributors
- She taps it and reads stories from Sarah's college friends
- She smiles and shows her husband

### Outcome
- Linda successfully shared her memory without any technical help after the initial QR scan
- She spent 7 minutes total, felt accomplished
- She tells other guests at her table how easy it was

## Journey 2: George's College Roommate Group

**8:17 PM - Tom's Submission**
- Tom writes: "George tried to impress Sarah by claiming he could surf. This man had never seen a surfboard in his life."
- Adds a photo of George face-planting in shallow water
- Submits successfully

**8:18 PM - Mike's Submission**

**8:19 PM - System Intelligence**

**8:20 PM - Real-time Discovery**
- Jake refreshes the album page
- Sees their stories are already grouped together
- "Dude, it knows these are all about the same trip!"

**8:21 PM - Adding More**
- Encouraged by the smart grouping, they add three more memories

- They spend the next 10 minutes browsing and laughing at other memories

### Outcome
- Five simultaneous users had no performance issues
- AI correctly grouped related memories in real-time
- The friends were impressed by the smart categorization
- They shared the link with other friends who couldn't attend

## Edge Cases & Error Journeys

### Failed Upload Journey
**Scenario**: Guest with poor connection
1. Types long memory
2. Adds 5 large photos
3. Hits submit
4. Loading spinner continues for 30 seconds
5. Error message: "Having trouble uploading. Your memory has been saved. Try again?"
6. Clicks "Try again"
7. System only uploads text first, queues photos
8. Success message: "Memory saved! Photos will be added when connection improves"
9. Photos upload later via background process

### Inappropriate Content Journey
**Scenario**: Drunk guest tries to share inappropriate story
1. Types inappropriate content
2. Hits submit
3. Gemini safety filter triggers
4. Message: "Let's keep memories family-friendly. Please revise and try again."
5. Content remains in form for editing
6. Guest revises or gives up

### Duplicate Story Journey
**Scenario**: Multiple people share same story
1. First person shares proposal story
2. Second person shares same story, different perspective
3. AI recognizes similarity
4. Groups under "The Proposal Story"
5. Third person shares same story
6. Also grouped automatically
7. Summary updates to incorporate all perspectives
8. Each maintains individual attribution

## Success Patterns

### What Makes Guests Share
1. **Clear value proposition** - They understand it's replacing the guest book
2. **Social proof** - Seeing others do it
3. **Easy access** - QR codes at every table
4. **No barriers** - No app, no login, no email required
5. **Immediate gratification** - See memory appear instantly
6. **Emotional trigger** - Wedding atmosphere encourages sharing

### What Prevents Frustration
1. **Fast loading** - Even on poor venue WiFi
2. **Clear CTAs** - Never unsure what to tap next
3. **Forgiving inputs** - No strict validation that blocks submission
4. **Progress indication** - Always know something is happening
5. **Graceful failures** - Never lose work, always recoverable

### What Delights Users
1. **Smart grouping** - "Wow, it knows these are related!"
2. **Beautiful presentation** - Looks professional and polished
3. **Instant sharing** - Can show others immediately
4. **Discovery** - Finding memories from other guests
5. **Permanence** - Knowing it's saved forever
